# Home Lab PC Hardware Inventory

This project documents a physical inspection of a desktop PC used for
home-lab learning and data-center hardware practice.

## Day 1 — Initial Inspection

The first day focused on identifying and documenting the PC hardware
without removing any components.

### Tasks Completed

- External inspection of the PC case
- Front and rear panel inspection
- Identification of USB, audio, VGA and Ethernet ports
- Identification of the PSU and power input
- Internal visual inspection of major hardware components
- Photographs taken for documentation

### Safety

- PC was powered off before inspection.
- Power cable was disconnected before opening the case.
- No components were removed during the initial inspection.
- The PSU was not opened.

## Hardware Identified

- Motherboard
- CPU cooler
- RAM
- HDD
- SSD
- PSU
- Dedicated graphics card
- SATA cables
- Power cables
- PCIe expansion slots
- Ethernet/LAN port
